01 / THE MISSION

Quantum shouldn't live
only in theory.

It should be something engineers can explore, researchers can test, and institutions can teach with confidence.

MQSI International Quantum Mission is an initiative led by the MQSI Advanced Research Community, powered by MAH Quantum and supported by partners. We help institutions make quantum technology a meaningful part of engineering education—not an isolated elective or a distant research topic.

Our approach connects curriculum planning, hands-on exploration, research direction and industry context, so learners can understand not only what quantum computing is, but what it looks like when real hardware runs their work.

02 / BEYOND SIMULATION

Not just a circuit on a screen.

Build the understanding to run a circuit, receive results from quantum hardware, and learn to interpret what happened.

Simulators are valuable teaching tools. Real quantum devices add another layer: the opportunity to observe physical hardware in action, account for noise and measurement, and study results that do not behave like an idealised model.

08 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations are part of good collaboration.

Does MQSI guarantee IBM quantum computer access?+

No. IBM independently evaluates Classroom Account requests and sets eligibility, approval and usage conditions. MQSI helps institutions plan curriculum integration and understand the pathway, but cannot promise approval or a particular device allocation.

Who can join the MQSI Advanced Research Community?+

University representatives, faculty, researchers, corporate participants and individual students can join the WhatsApp community to follow updates and engage with the wider community.

Who pays the US$500 annual subscription?+

The stated US$500 per year subscription is for universities and colleges joining the MQSI Mission as institutions. Students and individual community members do not need to pay this institutional subscription to join the WhatsApp group.

What does institutional participation involve?+

The mission is focused on curriculum integration, learning pathways, research and project engagement, industry context and community coordination. Detailed scope, terms and onboarding are confirmed directly with MAH Quantum before enrolment.

Can an institution apply if it is still developing its research capabilities?+

Yes, MQSI welcomes interest across different levels of institutional maturity, including Tier 3 through Tier 1 institutions. Any IBM Classroom Account request remains subject to IBM's own criteria and approval.
